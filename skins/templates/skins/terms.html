1. About Bonkverse

Bonkverse is a fan-made community platform built for players of the browser game bonk.io. It is independently developed and operated, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the creators of bonk.io. Bonkverse provides tools for discovering and sharing skins, tracking player stats, exploring community data, and enhancing the bonk.io experience through the browser extension.

Bonkverse is open source. The codebase is publicly available at github.com/orgs/Bonkverse.

2. Accounts

Bonkverse accounts are linked to your bonk.io account. Bonkverse facilitates authentication through bonk.io's own endpoints to verify your identity — we do not store your bonk.io password and do not own your bonk.io account. We are not responsible for unauthorized access to, theft of, or loss of your bonk.io account.

Allowed Alternate Accounts

You may use alternate bonk.io accounts ("alts") on Bonkverse. This helps us associate accounts that belong to the same player and improves the accuracy of our community data.

Prohibited Account Theft & Guest Accounts

You may not access, use, or impersonate another player's account without their explicit permission. bonk.io guest accounts are not permitted on Bonkverse.

Prohibited Impersonation

You may not impersonate another player, community figure, or Bonkverse staff member. Accounts found to be impersonating others may be removed.

Not Allowed Harmful or Offensive Public Content

Publicly visible content must not include: overtly NSFW or sexually explicit material, hate speech, racism, sexism, discrimination against any group of people, content that sexualizes minors, or content intended to harass, threaten, or harm any individual or group.

Note Community Standards Apply

We want Bonkverse to feel safe and welcoming for everyone. Content that causes genuine discomfort to community members — even if it falls in a grey area — may be reviewed and removed at staff discretion. When in doubt, keep it appropriate for a general audience.

Bonkverse is a platform open to everyone. We expect all users to treat each other with respect. The following behaviors are not tolerated:
