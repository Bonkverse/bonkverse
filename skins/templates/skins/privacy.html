Bonkverse ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website at bonkverse.io and the Bonkverse browser extension. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used. We are committed to transparency and only collect what is necessary to provide our services.

1. What Bonkverse Is

Bonkverse is a community platform for the browser game bonk.io. It provides a skin library where players can discover, upload, vote on, and apply skins; player profile and stats tracking; win/loss leaderboards; a Discord server directory; social stats; and a replay archive. The Bonkverse browser extension enhances the bonk.io game client with in-game overlays, kill tracking, and stat submission.

2. What We Collect
Website Bonk.io Account Identity

When you log in to Bonkverse, we collect your bonk.io username and a session token used to verify your identity and apply skins to your in-game skin slots. We do not collect or store your bonk.io password.

Website Player & Social Data

We collect bonk.io player usernames, friends list relationships, and associated game history to build player profiles and our community database. This data comes from the bonk.io platform when players connect their accounts.

Website Skin Uploads

When you upload a skin, we store the skin image data and associate it with your username to give proper credit. Uploaded skins are publicly visible on Bonkverse by default.

Website Game Replays

We collect bonk.io game replay data to build a searchable replay archive. Replays may include player usernames, match outcomes, and room metadata from public bonk.io games.

Extension In-Game Activity

The Bonkverse browser extension tracks in-game events including kills, deaths, wins, losses, and match outcomes while you play bonk.io. This data is sent to Bonkverse to populate your player profile and contribute to leaderboards. The extension only operates on bonk.io and does not monitor any other sites.

Extension Authentication Token

The extension stores your Bonkverse authentication token locally in your browser to keep you logged in between sessions. This token is never shared with third parties.

3. How We Use Your Data
  • To display your player profile, stats, and skin library on Bonkverse
  • To populate win/loss and skin leaderboards
  • To apply skins to your bonk.io skin slots when requested
  • To build a community player and replay database for the bonk.io community
  • To power in-game overlays and stats shown by the Bonkverse extension
  • To detect and moderate content that violates our community guidelines
4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your data with third parties except:

  • Service providers — hosting infrastructure (Railway) and CDN/security services (Cloudflare) necessary to operate Bonkverse. These providers process data only as needed to deliver our service.
  • Legal requirements — if required by law or to protect the safety of our community.
5. Data Retention

Player profile data, game stats, and uploaded skins are retained as long as your account is active or as long as they are useful for the community platform. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us. Replay data collected from public games may be retained for archival purposes.

6. AI Tools

We use AI tools including Claude (Anthropic) and ChatGPT (OpenAI) to assist in building and improving the Bonkverse platform. We do not share identifiable user data with these tools. Their use is limited to development assistance only.

7. Children's Privacy

Bonkverse is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Opt out of game stat submission by disabling the Bonkverse extension
9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as Bonkverse grows and new features are added. When we do, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change. Continued use of Bonkverse after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
