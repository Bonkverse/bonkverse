Bonkverse ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website at bonkverse.io and the Bonkverse browser extension. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used. We are committed to transparency and only collect what is necessary to provide our services.

1. What Bonkverse Is

Bonkverse is a community platform for the browser game bonk.io. It provides a skin library where players can discover, upload, vote on, and apply skins; player profile and stats tracking; skin leaderboards; a Discord server directory; social stats; a replay archive; and a public API that developers can use to show players' levels on their own sites and bots. The Bonkverse browser extension enhances the bonk.io game client with in-game overlays, kill tracking, and stat submission.

2. What We Collect

Website Bonk.io Account Identity

When you log in to Bonkverse, we collect your bonk.io username and a session token used to verify your identity and apply skins to your in-game skin slots. If you tick “Keep me connected for Wear”, we also keep bonk.io’s “remember me” token, so Bonkverse can get a new session token when yours expires without asking for your password again. Both tokens are stored encrypted, are never shared, and are deleted when you choose Disconnect in your settings (which also signs that token out on bonk.io). We do not collect or store your bonk.io password. If you log in with a friend request instead, we read the friend requests sent to Bonkverse's bonk.io account, note which account sent yours and when, and then delete the request; we never accept it.

If you add more than one bonk.io account, we record that they belong together so you can switch between them; they then share one vote per skin. Only you see that they're grouped unless you choose to list them on your profiles ("Also plays as"), and removing an account from the group in your settings undoes it. When your main account signs in on a browser, we set a cookie there with a random ID so that browser can switch between your accounts; we keep only a scrambled (hashed) copy of that ID, with a rough browser name (like “Chrome on Windows”) and when it was last used. You can remove a browser in your settings, and each is forgotten after 90 days unused. So the login page can offer “Choose an account” after you sign out, your browser also keeps a cookie listing the bonk.io names that signed in on it and how; nothing is stored on our side for that list, you can remove each name from that page, and ticking “Also forget this device” when signing out clears it. When an account signs in with its password or a friend request, we also note (with the same scrambled browser ID and rough browser name) that it may sign back in on that browser with one click for the next 30 days; you can end that from the login page, from your settings, or with “Also forget this device”.

Website Player & Social Data

We collect bonk.io player usernames, friends list relationships, and associated game history to build player profiles and our community database. This data comes from the bonk.io platform when players connect their accounts.

Website Skin Uploads

When you upload a skin, we store the skin image data and associate it with your username to give proper credit. Uploaded skins are publicly visible on Bonkverse by default. For skins submitted through BonxBot on Discord, we also store the Discord user ID of the person who submitted it.

Website Game Replays

We collect bonk.io game replay data to build a searchable replay archive. Replays may include player usernames, match outcomes, and room metadata from public bonk.io games.

Extension In-Game Activity

The Bonkverse browser extension tracks in-game events including kills, deaths, wins, losses, and match outcomes while you play bonk.io. This data is sent to Bonkverse to populate your player profile. The extension only operates on bonk.io and does not monitor any other sites.

Extension Authentication Token

The extension stores your Bonkverse authentication token locally in your browser to keep you logged in between sessions. This token is never shared with third parties.

Developers API Keys

When you create an API key on the developer portal, we store the name you give it, the contact you provide (such as a Discord username), when you accepted the API terms, and when the key was last used. We store only a hash of the key itself, so we cannot show it to you again.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To display your player profile, stats, and skin library on Bonkverse
  • To rank skins on the skin leaderboards
  • To apply skins to your bonk.io skin slots when requested
  • To build a community player and replay database for the bonk.io community
  • To power in-game overlays and stats shown by the Bonkverse extension
  • To detect and moderate content that violates our community guidelines

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your data with third parties except:

  • Service providers — hosting infrastructure (Railway) and CDN/security services (Cloudflare) necessary to operate Bonkverse. These providers process data only as needed to deliver our service.
  • The public API — developers with an API key can read players' levels, level history and game counts collected from public bonk.io replays, the estimated account age, and an image of the skin a player wears in game. The API never returns skin codes or the XP from your own bonk.io login. If you hide the Level section in your profile settings, the API does not return your level either. Developers agree to the API terms, which forbid reselling the data.
  • Legal requirements — if required by law or to protect the safety of our community.

5. Data Retention

Player profile data, game stats, and uploaded skins are retained as long as your account is active or as long as they are useful for the community platform. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us. Replay data collected from public games may be retained for archival purposes.

6. AI Tools

We use AI tools including Claude (Anthropic) and ChatGPT (OpenAI) to assist in building and improving the Bonkverse platform. We do not share identifiable user data with these tools. Their use is limited to development assistance only.

7. Children's Privacy

Bonkverse is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Opt out of game stat submission by disabling the Bonkverse extension

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as Bonkverse grows and new features are added. When we do, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change. Continued use of Bonkverse after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
